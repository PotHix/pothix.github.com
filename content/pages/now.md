This is my [now page](http://nownownow.com/about), inspired by [Derek Sivers](https://sivers.org/now).

I'm currently living in Indaiatuba, São Paulo and working remotely. I have [my own company](https://aule.software), work full-time for [Doist](https://doist.com) and sporadically to [Intervac](https://intervac-homeexchange.com) when I have time to spare.

I'm excited and concerned about the changes happening before LLM. Excited because I'm able to make so many things happening now! It's incredible how much the productivity increased, and how many new projects that wouldn't see the light of day are now being done. Concerned about the many changes to many things, including my profession. It's crazy to look back one year and see how much it changed, and then look to the future and not have a clue of what it will be.

That said, I'm still taking care of Doist's APIs and modernizing the codebase, which is something I always enjoying. Last quarter I worked on improving the tooling and improving performance, which is also something I quite enjoy.

I'm forcing myself to get back to the gym, and doing more exercises in general. I'm improving my diet and was able to lose 4kg in the past 2 months. It's not a lot, but I'm not in a hurry.

Family is great and I'm enjoying my life with my kids (twins, 6yo). The latest one was playing Pokemon TCG with them.

The only hobby I'm able to keep up nowadays is playing [Magic the gathering (Pauper)](/mtg) at least once a month. In paper, in a local game store, to fully disconnect from everything.

There are some things I'm still involved with, but not very active:

1. Organizing the [São Paulo Rust community](http//www.meetup.com/Rust-Sao-Paulo-Meetup/) 🦀 (since 2017) and the [Ruby community in São Paulo](https://gurusp.org) (since 2008!). I miss meetups and speaking, but I can't really justify it right now.
2. Custom computer keyboards in general. I'm still trying to find some time to fix my fully assembled [dactyl-cc](https://github.com/mjohns/dactyl-cc/) and post about it, and also trying to find time to build my Corne low profile keyboard. I have half of it working, but never finished 😅.
